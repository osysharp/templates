## What is free

- **Local development is free** and needs no account. `osy` builds, tests and runs your app on your own machine.
- **Every application starts free on Osyrin Cloud.** You need no card and no plan to choose. The free tier is a real production deployment: the same compiler, security model, durable execution and audit trail a paid application has.

Osyrin Cloud is in beta, and nothing can be bought yet.

## How pricing will work

When an application outgrows the free tier, it is charged by what it runs. It is never charged per person, per seat, per developer or per account, so a tool the whole company uses costs the same as one three people use.

Prices are announced at general availability. Until then we do not quote numbers. If you need one to plan with, write to contact@osyrin.com and we will talk it through.

## Limits

Every application is held to fair-use limits on compute, storage and the work it may have in flight, so no application can take the machine from the rest. The limits are published before Osyrin Cloud opens, on the Plans page at osyrin.com.

- A published limit is the limit in force today.
- A limit only ever rises without notice. It falls only with 30 days' notice, on the Plans page.
- An application that hits a limit keeps running, and we tell you. Nothing is cut off without warning, and nothing is deleted for going over.

## AI costs

Whatever your application asks an AI model to do, it asks with your own key, and the provider bills you. We do not resell tokens.

## An application nobody uses

After 90 days with no visit, no deploy and no sign-in, an application is marked dormant and you get an email. Any visit, deploy or sign-in resets the clock.

If it is still untouched 30 days after that email, the application and its data are removed. A final backup is taken first and kept for 30 more days, and you can download it from the admin.

## What counts as one application

One deployed Osyrin application: its data, its users and its own address. A page, a service or a copy running for testing is not a separate application.

## You own what you build

An Osyrin application is a folder of source you hold, on any plan, from the first day.
